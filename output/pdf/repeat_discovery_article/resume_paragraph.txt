Developed a reproducible bioinformatics benchmarking pipeline for identifying related families of repeated DNA sequences. Applied transfer learning with a pretrained Nucleotide Transformer, supervised contrastive learning, nonlinear embedding projection, and selective fine-tuning using PyTorch and Hugging Face Transformers. Implemented reverse-complement augmentation, GC-aware negative sampling, AdamW optimization, and mixed-precision GPU training. Compared neural embeddings with canonical k-mer features using HDBSCAN clustering, FAISS similarity search, and Leiden graph analysis. Developed a complementary genome-search pipeline using canonical 9-mer seeding, triplet-based statistical profiles, and affine-gap refinement. Designed family-disjoint train/validation/test splits, validation-based hyperparameter and checkpoint selection, and evaluation with pairwise precision, recall, F1, and adjusted Rand index; documented comparative results, error analysis, and limitations.
